Loryana Clarke

Architecture Student & Creative Thinker

I am a junior at Arizona State University pursuing a bachelor's degree in Architecture with a minor in Business. I am passionate about exploring the human experience and how we can manipulate the interaction and outcomes we have.

My Journey

My interest in architecture comes from my curiosity of the world. I am interested in how people interact with the spaces around them. I enjoy discovering how thoughtful design can influence everyday experiences and create meaningful connections between people and their environments.

Design Interests

I am drawn to contemporary and biophilic design. I enjoy exploring natural materials, light, sustainability, and the relationship between architecture and nature.

Beyond Architecture

Outside of school, I enjoy traveling, photography, reading, sewing, and being outdoors. These interests allow me to explore creativity from different perspectives and bring new inspiration into my design work.

Future Aspirations

My goal is to become a licensed architect and continue expanding my knowledge through new experiences, collaboration, and opportunities within the architecture field.
