Room of Reflection

ARCHITECTURE · 2026

The purpose of this project was to reimagine the welcome space at Pioneer and Military Memorial Park, creating an environment that fosters reflection, peace, and a seamless connection to the surrounding cemetery.

The Room of Reflection was designed as a tranquil, shaded space where visitors could pause, reflect, and honor those who have passed, encouraging a deeper connection between the visitor, the landscape, and the history of the site.
