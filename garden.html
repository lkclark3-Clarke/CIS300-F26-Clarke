Sun, Water, and Earth Gardens

ARCHITECTURE · 2025

This project explored the relationship between fundamental architectural elements—points, lines, and planes—and their corresponding forms of columns, walls, and slabs. These elements were used to develop three distinct architectural gardens: Sun, Water, and Earth. Each garden explored a different environmental condition through spatial organization, material relationships, and the interaction between architecture and the landscape. Together, the gardens investigated how elemental architectural forms can shape our experience of light, water, and ground.

Sun Garden

The Sun Garden explored the interplay of light, shadow, shade, and darkness through architectural elements, revealing how sunlight transforms spatial experiences throughout the garden.

Water Garden

The Water Garden explored the movement and management of water through detention, flooding, flow, and diversion, investigating how architectural elements can guide, collect, and redirect water through the landscape.

Earth Garden

The Earth Garden explored the transformation of the ground through retaining, cutting, caving, and filling, investigating how shaping the earth creates varied spatial conditions and relationships between architecture and landscape.
